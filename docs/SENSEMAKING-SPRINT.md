# The Sensemaking Sprint (working title) — a co-design brief

**Status:** proposal for Pod Squad co-design — nothing here is a decree except the section marked "Fixed."
**Owner:** hello@brendanwhitaker.com · **Drafted:** July 2026 · **Lives:** this doc is the Pod Squad's to rewrite (PRs welcome; ✨ sprint-idea issues for anything you want to discuss first).

---

## What this event is

The first in-person experience after the Kickoff Summit — the room where the cohort's field evidence becomes shared maps. Participants arrive having distributed the field survey and (ideally) extracted their harvest into source-extract cards; they leave having sorted their pools, built their first Evidence on the canvas, unlocked the full ladder together, and — for tables that move fast — mapped a first draft Problem Situation. It is the moment the cycle pivots from *collecting* to *making sense*.

It is **not** the day pods form, paradoxes get finished, or anything gets decided. Forcing convergence on day one is precisely the failure mode the method exists to prevent.

## Q1 — the name (yes, the name is co-design question #1)

This event slot has carried three names:

1. **Problem Sprint** — the name on the public calendar (July 28, DC Public Library) and in the Open Cycle Agreement's presence commitment.
2. **Paradox Sprint** — the owner's July design docs renamed it, on the argument that *"the paradox is the point."*
3. **Sensemaking Sprint** — the current working title, on the argument that participants at this event are nowhere near a paradox yet; what they're actually doing all day is *sensemaking*, and "problematization day" (an earlier internal name) was accurate but unlovable.

The tension is real: name it for what happens in the room (sensemaking) or for what the whole arc is aiming at (the paradox)? **Squad call.** Whatever you choose propagates to the calendar, the agreement copy, and these docs.

## Fixed (not up for co-design)

- **The calendar slot:** July 28, 2026, DC Public Library — Main branch (anchor event #2 of six; attendance is part of the presence commitment). *The format of the slot is Q2 below.*
- **The cohort:** ~24–40 participants, in pods-to-be of tables.
- **The instrument:** this tool, in the browser, on whatever devices we settle (Q7). It works offline; venue wifi is not a dependency.
- **The BYO-LLM rule:** no in-app AI, ever. Any AI assistance at the event is participants running copied prompts in their own models.
- **The survey precedes it:** the field survey is live from Kickoff; the sprint works whatever harvest exists by then.
- **The posture:** Poderators shepherd; nobody grades anything.

## Goals / anti-goals

**People leave with:**
- their pool **sorted** (every extract decided: signal / noise / super),
- **first Evidence cards** built and named — the connect gesture in their hands,
- the **Unlock moment** experienced together (see run-of-show — it's a room ritual, not a settings toggle),
- fast tables: a **draft Problem Situation** mapped, and a taste of the workbook,
- their board **exported and committed** to a shared repo (or a clear path to do it that night),
- the date and shape of **Meet the Pods** (Aug 18) in their heads.

**Explicit anti-goals:**
- ❌ a finished paradox (Stage 5 is weeks of work; today plants the question),
- ❌ pod formation (that's the arc's later beat — see Q6),
- ❌ complete evidence coverage (thin is honest; the tool exports "What we're still testing" on purpose),
- ❌ anyone feeling graded.

## Preconditions checklist

- [ ] Survey harvest reviewed: how many responses exist, and are they moderated/exportable? (Q8)
- [ ] **Extraction decision made** (Q4): did participants extract before the event, or is extraction Block 2?
- [ ] Per-table CSV packs prepared from the harvest (`title,summary[,source_url]`) — the day-one bridge from OLOS to the tool (Q8).
- [ ] Devices confirmed (Q7) — the tool runs on phones, but the canvas rewards a laptop/tablet.
- [ ] Each table's shared repo created (or one cohort repo with per-table folders), Poderators have write access, one Poderator has done the web-upload path end-to-end.
- [ ] Projector + one facilitator board for the live demo beats.

## Draft run-of-show — full day (~6 hours)

*A proposal to react to, not a script to obey. Times assume 10:00–16:30 with lunch. Each block names the tool state the room should be in — the action bar shows it, so Poderators can see at a glance who's where.*

| Time | Block | Tool state | What happens | Poderator move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | **Arrival & the inversion** (30m) | intro deck on the projector | The one story that frames the day: *Dorst gets handed a problem by a client. We don't have a client — we have a field. Today the evidence gets to speak first.* Show the arc: today → Meet the Pods → Frame Sprint. | Set the no-grading tone. Say the localStorage gotcha out loud ("your board lives in THIS browser on THIS device"). |
| 10:30 | **Load & sort sprint** (45m) | `Sources` → `Sort` | Tables load their CSV packs (or the sample as warm-up). Solo, fast, phones fine. Race the counter, not each other. | Narrate hesitation as data. Watch for over-deliberators; "sort fast, trust the loop." |
| 11:15 | *Break* (15m) | — | — | Scan the room's progress chips. |
| 11:30 | **Triangulate (Seed Mode)** (75m) | `Validate` — seed | Pairs at tables. Glowing dots → first Evidence cards; name every claim. The concept invite will appear for some (~10 kept) — name it or dismiss it, both are right. | The connect gesture is the #1 stall — demo it once on the projector, then roam. Enforce naming: an unnamed card is a hunch wearing a costume. |
| 12:45 | **The Unlock moment** (10m) | the graduate sheet | The room ritual: everyone unlocks Patterns & Themes *together*, on a count. One-way, all-in, applause encouraged. This is the day's threshold — mark it. | Frame it: "your hunches come with you; what unlocks is the ability to claim more." |
| 12:55 | *Lunch* (45m) | — | — | — |
| 13:40 | **Climb & map** (60m) | `Map` | Evidence → Patterns (name the mechanism) → a Theme (named + described) → the bar offers **Map the Problem Situation**. Fast tables map; others keep climbing — both are on pace. | The locks will bite here. Read the lock text with people — every lock names its syndrome. Don't rescue; translate. |
| 14:40 | **Classify** (30m) | the classify beat | Tables that mapped take the guided seven-types walk (one type at a time, Dorst's order). Others continue climbing. | Walk one type on the projector. "Where is your coverage thin?" is the takeaway, not completeness. |
| 15:10 | **Deepen — a taste** (30m) | `Deepen`, Stages 1 & 5 preview | Stage 1 (solidify) for real; then *open* Stage 5 and read the paradox stage together — the question the next three weeks answer. Do not fill it in today. | Plant cui bono: "when you do find your paradox, the test is *who benefits from it persisting*." |
| 15:40 | **Ship & close** (35m) | Share / Export | Every board exported (working folder), one live demo of the web-upload commit, every table's work in the repo before they leave (or pledged for tonight). Preview Meet the Pods: what a deck+site looks like (show one). What happens between now and Aug 18. | The Git demo is yours. End on the loop: "everything you made today is revisable — that's the design." |

**Total hands-on-tool time: ~4h20m.** Rooms run 20% slower than run-of-shows believe; the classify and deepen blocks are the designated sacrifices if the day runs long.

## Open co-design questions — the Squad owns these

*Claim one by opening a ✨ sprint-idea issue referencing its number.*

- **Q1 — The name.** Problem Sprint / Paradox Sprint / Sensemaking Sprint (see above).
- **Q2 — The slot vs. the format.** The calendar currently books this as an **evening event (18:00)**; this brief drafts a **full day**. Reconcile: move the slot, split into an evening + a Saturday, or compress the run-of-show to ~2.5 evening hours (what gets cut)?
- **Q3 — Solo boards vs. shared.** Draft assumes solo boards with pair-work at tables, merged via Git afterward. Alternative: one board per table on a shared machine from the start. When does the first *merge* happen — at the sprint, or async in week 3?
- **Q4 — Extraction: before or during?** Homework-before keeps the day for sensemaking but risks uneven arrival states; a live extraction block (BYO-LLM at the venue) equalizes but eats ~45 minutes and needs devices+connectivity for the AI step (the tool itself is offline; their LLM isn't).
- **Q5 — What must a pod present at Meet the Pods?** No deliverable spec exists anywhere in the platform docs. The tool's **deck & site export is the de-facto candidate** — is it *the* requirement, a floor, or optional? What are the minimum honest sections (the export already carries "How we know" and "What we're still testing" — is that the bar)?
- **Q6 — Pod formation mechanics.** The owner's design model forms pods by *clustering* (merge canvases → cluster hypotheses → second → vote; clusters clearing pod_min = 12 become pods; ~2–3 pods from a 24–40 cohort). The older shipped spec forms pods from *voted problem statements* (pod_min = 5, up to 8 pods). Which mechanics run this cycle, and what part happens in/around this event vs. Meet the Pods?
- **Q7 — Devices.** BYO-laptop, a library laptop cart, tablets, phones-only tables? (Sorting is great on phones; the canvas wants bigger glass.)
- **Q8 — The OLOS → tool bridge, day-of.** For now: organizers export survey responses to per-table CSVs by hand. Who owns preparing them, what's the moderation bar for what's included, and do we want the tool to ingest a survey export format directly (file a ✨ if yes — it's buildable)?
