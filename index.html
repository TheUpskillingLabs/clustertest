How to use the AI

This tool generates prompts — you take them to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or whatever you use. Don't accept the first answer. Ask "what am I missing?" and "what's the strongest argument against this?" The ✨ See the whole pool button in the header is your best starting move.

Connect two signals to create a Pattern. Connect two patterns to create a Theme. Connect two themes to create a Super-theme. Each level goes deeper.
