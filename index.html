01Why it matters

The most powerful move isn't a better solution. It's a better question.

When a problem keeps swallowing solutions, the answer isn't to try harder. It's to look differently. Every unsolvable challenge is stuck inside a frame — an unexamined way of seeing that everyone inherited and no one questioned. Crack it open, and solutions you couldn't have imagined start to feel inevitable.

This is the move that separates people who manage problems from people who solve them. Kees Dorst called it Frame Creation. You're about to do it.

02How it works

See what others miss. Say what no one else could.

  1. 01

    Sort the pool

    Work through your observations one at a time. Keep what's real, discard the noise, flag the ones that might shift everything. Fast and instinctive — trust your gut, revisit later.

  2. 02

    Triangulate evidence

    Two signals pointing at the same thing? That's evidence. Connect them on the canvas, classify the claim using Dorst's seven types, and name what you've found. Now you can defend it.

  3. 03

    Climb the ladder

    Evidence into pattern. Pattern into theme. Each step, the real problem comes into focus. Each tier unlocks only when the level below is named — because every claim should rest on examined ones.

  4. 04

    Name the situation

    When the map is complete, push it forward. Synthesize the open condition your frame will come from. This is where your breakthrough lives — and the evidence trail makes every word of it yours to stand behind.
