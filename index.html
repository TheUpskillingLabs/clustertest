What to Do Next: AI Sparring Partners

Since you already use a Google account to log into OLOS, you can use these prompts completely free with Google Gemini or NotebookLM (which is awesome for uploading notes and deep research). You can also paste them into ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity.

1. Who is Involved? (Stakeholder Mapping)

Find the hidden players, helpers, and gatekeepers trapped in your problem space.

Act as an expert UX researcher and systems thinker. I have created a problem space cluster called: [INSERT CLUSTER TITLE] It is defined by this shared barrier: [INSERT DESCRIPTION/SYNTHESIS] Help me build a robust Stakeholder Map for this space. Do not try to solve the problem. Instead, identify: 1. The Primary Affected Stakeholder (Who feels the pain most directly?) 2. Adjacent Stakeholders (Who else is impacted downstream?) 3. Gatekeepers / Blockers (Who can constrain, slow, or prevent progress in this space?) 4. Enablers / Intermediaries (Who has the access or trust to reach the affected stakeholders?)

2. Challenge Your Assumptions (Problematization)

Look beneath the surface to find why this problem stays broken.

Act as a critical theorist and problem-space analyst. I have created a problem space cluster called: [INSERT CLUSTER TITLE] It is defined by this shared barrier: [INSERT DESCRIPTION/SYNTHESIS] Help me problematize this space. Your goal is to complicate my understanding and look for systemic roots. 1. What are the hidden assumptions baked into how I have defined this problem? 2. If this problem persists, who currently benefits from the status quo? 3. Are there any structural biases (economic, racial, geographic) embedded in how this problem is framed?

3. Find Real Proof (Empirical Research & Literature Plans)

Build a rapid interview and citation lookup checklist to prove your theory stands up.

System Insight: Running this specific prompt inside Google NotebookLM or Perplexity AI works incredibly well for finding real scientific papers and news.

Act as an expert research strategist and data validation expert. I have created a problem space cluster called: [INSERT CLUSTER TITLE] It is defined by this shared barrier: [INSERT DESCRIPTION/SYNTHESIS] I need to validate and complicate this problem space before moving forward. Help me design a rapid, rigorous investigation plan using primary methods (interviews, surveys) and secondary methods (peer-reviewed research, literature). Please provide: 1. Complicating Factors: What edge cases, missing voices, or contrarian data might prove my framing wrong or incomplete? 2. Secondary Research: What specific academic fields, peer-reviewed topics, or existing literature should I quickly search to ground this in established evidence? 3. Rapid Primary Research Plan: Give me 3 highly targeted interview questions or survey concepts I could deploy this week to test my core assumptions with real stakeholders.
